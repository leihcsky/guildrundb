Guildrun shops are where runs are won. Combat is the report card; **Shards** are the test. This guide answers one question for Demo **0.5.6**:

> On this screen, should I buy a **Rank**, a **Relic**, or an **Item**?

It is not a tier list. It is a priority filter. Use it after you know *why* you are weak — if you just wiped, run [Why did I wipe?](/guides/fight-loss-checklist) first.

## The default priority (when nothing is on fire)

Use this order unless a section below overrides it:

1. **Survive the next opener** — a body or placement that stops the carry dying first.
2. **Rank the win condition once** — usually one carry or one wall to **B**.
3. **Enable your keywords** — a relic or item that makes Rush / Stall / Crit / Shield actually fire.
4. **Amplify** — second relic, luxury Unique, off-plan items.
5. **Horizonal greed** — extra heroes, speculative Uniques, even spreads across the roster.

Most lost runs invert this list: Unique first, Rank never, opener still naked.

## Rank — when the level-up is the spike

**Buy Rank when:**

- One hero clearly wins fights *if they live and cast* (examples: [Nyx](/heroes/nyx), [Irini](/heroes/irini), [Aria](/heroes/aria), [Funke](/heroes/funke)).
- You are approaching a challenge / boss node and still have all C-ranks.
- A Rank B specialization path is the actual build (execute tribal, Rush AS, Shield wall) and you already know which branch you want.

**Skip Rank when:**

- Nobody can tank the entry hex — Rank on a dead carry is a donation.
- You are one enabler away from making your relics work (for example you own Rush payoffs but nobody triggers Rush).
- You would Rank a flex unit “because Shards were burning” while the carry stays C.

**Practical rule:** One intentional **B** beats three accidental C→C cosmetic upgrades. Look up the hero’s specialization notes on their page before you lock the branch.

## Relic — when the shop piece is the plan

**Buy a Relic when:**

- It is an **engine or banner** for a class/keyword you already drafted (see [Reading relic offers](/guides/reading-relic-offers)).
- It fixes the failure sentence from the last fight (“Rush never fired,” “no Shield on contact”).
- It is a cheap Common/Rare enabler that turns on an expensive Unique you already took.

**Skip a Relic when:**

- The condition will not happen most fights (on-kill with no execute, Stall with a pure race board, class banner with one dabbler).
- You still have zero Rank spike and the next node is a wall.
- You are buying Unique rarity to feel progress while the opener checklist is red.

**Practical rule:** Every relic should answer “who triggers this every combat?” If you cannot answer, it is not a shop priority — it is a trap with good art.

Browse by tag when stuck: [Rush](/keywords/rush), [Stall](/keywords/stall), [Crit](/keywords/crit), [Shield](/keywords/shield), or the full [Relics](/relics) index. Each relic page’s **Shop decision** block is written for this exact moment.

## Item — when gear beats another relic

**Buy an Item when:**

- Your carry’s job is clear and the item’s stats/keywords are that job (Crit on a Crit carry, Defense on the wall, Attack Speed on a stacker).
- You need a small, reliable swing this fight and relics on offer are off-plan.
- A relic condition is already online and the item multiplies it (for example Crit item after Crit engines).

**Skip an Item when:**

- You are itemizing a hero who still dies first — placement/tank first.
- The item is off-class greed while a class-tagged piece for your actual carry is available.
- You have no Rank B and no keyword engine; another shiny stick will not invent a win condition.

**Practical rule:** Items are **precision tools**. Relics are often **identity**. Ranks are **breakpoints**. If your identity is missing, itemizing C-ranks is polishing a draft that does not exist yet.

Use [Items](/items) and the item page **Shop decision** notes the same way you use relic notes.

## Overrides — ignore the default order

### You are dying on contact

Priority becomes: **formation → tank hero/relic → then Rank the carry**.  
Do not Rank the carry into the same dive tile. Do not Unique into a collapsing board.

### Your keywords are dead

Priority becomes: **enabler relic/item → then Rank**.  
A Rank B Nyx with zero Rush support can still feel weak; a Rank C with working Rush can clear the next pack.

### The next node is a known spike (challenge / boss)

Priority becomes: **one Rank B or one hard enabler**, not a wide shop of maybes. Spend like you expect this fight to end the run if you whiff.

### You are flooded with Shards late

Priority becomes: **finish the carry’s item slots and on-plan Uniques**, not a sixth hero who dilutes class banners. Extra bodies are not automatic strength in Guildrun.

### Dual-class temptation ([Tilly](/heroes/tilly), [Rowan](/heroes/rowan), [Zuri](/heroes/zuri))

Priority becomes: **commit one fantasy** before buying every dual-tagged offer. Shop order fails when every relic is “technically legal” and none are online together.

## A simple shop script (30 seconds)

1. Say the failure or goal in one line (“opener,” “need Rush,” “Rank Irini”).
2. Scan offers that **answer that line** only.
3. If Rank is that answer and the hero will live — Rank.
4. Else if a relic enables or is the identity — Relic.
5. Else if an item sharpens the same job — Item.
6. Else reroll / leave / take economy — do not force a off-plan Unique.

## Examples (Demo mindset)

**Example A — Carry dies first, you have a Warrior in shop and a Unique Crit relic.**  
Buy or place the wall / move the carry. The Unique waits. Rank the carry only after they stop eating the opener.

**Example B — Board lives, Rush relics do nothing, Irini is still C.**  
Buy a Rush enabler or Attack Speed that makes Rush real, *or* Rank Irini if her path is the Rush stacker and she already triggers it. Do not add a Stall Unique “for value.”

**Example C — Keywords online, fight length is fine, challenge next.**  
Rank B on the carry or wall. Items that complete the same plan are fine. Off-class banners are not.

## FAQ

### Should I always Rank before relics?

No. Rank is the default *breakpoint* when the hero is the plan and can live. Enabler relics beat Rank when your paid keywords are inert.

### Are Uniques ever early buys?

Yes — when they *are* the enabler or the only way to stabilize (rare). Most early Uniques are ego buys. Read Skip when on the relic page.

### What if the shop shows nothing on-plan?

Reroll if the economy allows, take a flexible Common enabler, or leave gold on a future node. Taking a random Epic to “use the shop” is how identities rot.

### How does this work with published builds?

[Builds](/builds) tell you a target shape. This guide tells you the **order** to buy pieces when the shop is incomplete. Follow the build’s gameplan steps, not every listed relic the moment it appears.

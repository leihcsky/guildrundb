Guildrun runs are a growth problem disguised as a shop problem. You do not need every hero at Rank S. You need the **right breakpoints** at the **right board size** so the next fight is solvable. This **Growth Route** guide is the Demo **0.5.7** manual for that path.

Use it when you are stuck deciding:

- Rank this hero or recruit another?
- Specialize now or stabilize first?
- Expand the field or finish the carry?
- Take Rank A/S greed or fix the missing job?

Shard **priority inside one shop screen** still lives on [Shop order](/guides/shop-order-shards). This page is the **route across the whole run**.

## The growth route at a glance

| Stage | Board feeling | Default action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1 · Opening** | Jobs unclear, everyone C | Cover wall + damage; do not greed copies |
| **2 · First spike** | One hero is carrying fights | First intentional **Rank B** on that hero |
| **3 · Expansion** | One fantasy works, other jobs missing | Add a **new job**, not a fourth clone |
| **4 · Engine** | Combat is stable enough | Lock a relic / modifier line that fires every fight |
| **5 · Final shape** | Challenge / boss pressure | Finish the carry; last slot answers the matchup |

That is the same five-step strip on the [homepage](/) Growth Route — this article is the full decision text behind it.

## Rank ladder: what you are actually buying

| Rank | What it usually means | Buy when… | Skip when… |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **C** | Base kit | The hero already has a useful job on the board | You drafted a name you cannot place or feed |
| **B** | One of three **specializations** (some add a class) | This hero is already winning fights *if they live* | Nobody can tank; Rank on a corpse is a donation |
| **A** | Package + first **class modifier** from the active pool | Your engine is online and the modifier will trigger often | You still lack Rank B on the win condition |
| **S** | Demo ceiling: second package / modifier (+ item flexibility) | Finishing this carry beats expanding again | A missing job or dead keyword still explains losses |

Open any hero page (for example [Irini](/heroes/irini), [Aria](/heroes/aria), [Fiona](/heroes/fiona)) to see Rank art and specialization names before you lock a branch.

**Hard rule:** one intentional **B** beats three accidental cosmetic upgrades. Dual-class Rank B paths change later modifier pools — treat them as route choices, not cosmetics.

## Stage 1 — Opening (mostly C)

**Goal:** survive and learn, not invent the final Endless board.

- Field **one wall** and **one clear damage dealer**.
- Optional third: sustain, control, or economy — not a second glass carry.
- Keep formations simple: wall front, carry back. Details: [Player Handbook](/guides/player-handbook).

**Do not:**

- fill the board with four copies of the same fantasy,
- Rank randomly because Shards were burning,
- take a Unique that needs a tag nobody triggers yet.

If you want a worked example of job coverage, skim [Builds](/builds) or the [Tier List](/tier-list) — as templates, not scripts.

## Stage 2 — First spike (first Rank B)

**Goal:** create a breakpoint the next gate fight can feel.

**Specialize the hero who is already doing the job**, not your favorite portrait.

Good first B targets:

- the carry who wins when they live ([Nyx](/heroes/nyx), [Irini](/heroes/irini), [Aria](/heroes/aria), …),
- or the wall who enables that carry ([Pimenta](/heroes/pimenta), [Reyna](/heroes/reyna), [Rip](/heroes/rip), …) if openers still delete the backline.

**Skip first B when** the wipe sentence is still “carry ate first hit.” Fix placement / wall first ([fight-loss checklist](/guides/fight-loss-checklist)).

## Stage 3 — Expansion (new job, not new clone)

**Goal:** unlock coverage the seed is asking for.

Ask one question before every recruit:

> What **new job** does this hero add?

| Symptom | Expand toward |
| --- | --- |
| Carry dies first | Real wall / peel ([Tank](/classes/tank), [Vanguard](/classes/vanguard)) |
| Fights end before casts | Mana / Stall enabler, or safer geometry |
| Rush never fires | Rush partner or duration tools — see [Rush vs Stall](/guides/rush-vs-stall) |
| Long fights, no sustain | [Mystic](/classes/mystic) shields / recovery |
| Need execute / pick | Assassin flex — only after a wall exists |

**Anti-pattern:** fourth Duelist before any wall. That is collection, not growth.

Board-size greed (paying to field another body) only pays if the new slot has a job **ready now**. An empty hex with a C-rank tourist is worse than a tight three with a real B spike.

## Stage 4 — Engine (modifier / relic identity)

**Goal:** name the run out loud and stop drafting against it.

Examples of engines:

- Rush Duelists + Acceleration-style payoffs
- Stall Mage + Mage / Stall banners
- Shield Warrior / Mystic sustain

Rules:

1. Every paid keyword needs a hero who triggers it **most fights**.
2. Prefer an enabler Common/Rare over a second Unique with the same dead condition.
3. Class modifiers at Rank A should fire often — cute once-per-boss text is usually wrong.

Learn offer language in [Banner vs Engine vs Unique](/guides/reading-relic-offers). Browse tags on [Keywords](/keywords) when the shop confuses you.

## Stage 5 — Final shape

**Goal:** convert remaining Shards into the fight in front of you.

- Finish the carry (Rank / items) once the wall and engine exist.
- Use the **last slot** for matchup answers (extra Frost, Burn, peel, economy), not a random fourth fantasy.
- Before challenge / boss / [Red Rift](/guides/red-rift) pressure: empty the wallet on power you can use **this** fight.

If you still do not know whether to Rank, Relic, or Item on the current screen, open [Shop order](/guides/shop-order-shards).

## Symptom table (use after every loss)

Write one wipe sentence, then pick the growth action:

| Wipe sentence | Growth action |
| --- | --- |
| “Carry died first” | Placement / wall — not more damage Rank |
| “Nobody cast” | Survive + Mana/tempo — then Rank the caster |
| “Rush/Stall never fired” | Enabler hero or relic — see tempo guide |
| “We have three of the same job” | Expand a missing job or cut a clone |
| “Board feels weak but wallet is full” | Intentional Rank B on the win condition |
| “Engine is online, bosses still race us” | Finish carry (A/S / items) or add sustain flex |

Changing placement, ranks, *and* relics in one shop teaches nothing. Change the row that matches the sentence.

## Closing mindset

Growth in Guildrun is **serial**, not parallel. Stabilize jobs → spike one hero → add missing coverage → lock an engine → finish the plan. Players who upgrade everything a little stay permanently almost-ready for the next gate.

Walk one calm Demo 0.5.7 clear with this route. When losses become explainable (“wrong job,” “early B on the wrong unit,” “expanded before the spike”), you are ready for harder rungs — not when you simply want a louder badge.

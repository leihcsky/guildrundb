The relic shop in Guildrun is noisy on purpose. Rarity looks like power; **conditions** are power. This guide teaches you to read an offer in Demo **0.5.3** the way a strong run does: name role → keyword → trigger frequency → then rarity.

Pair it with:

- [Shop order](/guides/shop-order-shards) — whether a relic should beat Rank/Item this screen
- [Fight-loss checklist](/guides/fight-loss-checklist) — if you are buying to fix a wipe
- [Relics](/relics) database — effect text, tags, and Shop decision notes on each page
- [Keywords](/keywords) — what your board can actually trigger

## The 10-second read

For every relic on offer, answer in order:

1. **What is its job title?** (banner, engine, filigree/prism, amplifier, conduit/stacker, quest/boost, enemy reference)
2. **Which keywords or classes does it pay?**
3. **How often will that trigger in *my* next three fights?**
4. **Is the rarity charging me for a condition I do not meet?**

If step 3 is “rarely,” skip — even if step 4 says Unique.

## Name roles (what the title is trying to tell you)

Guildrun relic names are not random flavor. Patterns repeat across classes. Treat them as **role labels**.

### Banner — class or identity flag

**Usually means:** “Commit to this class / fantasy.” Endurance, Patience, Outbreak, Greed-style banners want a real density of that class or keyword, not a single tourist hero.

**Take when:** You already have two pieces of the fantasy (heroes + another enabler) or the banner *is* the missing enabler for a committed board.

**Skip when:** You own one Assassin and the rest is Mage soup. Banners punish dabbling.

### Engine — fight-to-fight loop

**Usually means:** A payoff machine (Shield loops, Ambush executes, Bloodshield-style sustain). Engines want repetition: every combat, not once per act.

**Take when:** The loop’s fuel is already on the board (hits taken, kills, Rush procs, casts).

**Skip when:** You are hoping the engine will create the kills or Shields by itself.

### Filigree / Prism — power spike modules

**Usually means:** Mid–late amplifiers once the identity exists. They raise a working plan; they rarely invent one.

**Take when:** Banner/engine (or hero kit) already fires and you need a spike for a hard node.

**Skip when:** You still fail the fight-loss opener or keyword checklist.

### Amplifier — clean multipliers

**Usually means:** Broad martial or defensive multipliers. Flexible, but still asks your board to be doing that thing.

**Take when:** You have a simple Attack / Defense / keyword plan and need a reliable bump.

**Skip when:** You need a specialist enabler, not another generic multiplier.

### Conduit / Accumulator / stacker — frequency junkies

**Usually means:** Value scales with how often a keyword triggers across the run or fight.

**Take when:** Your board is a machine gun for that tag (Rush every fight, kills every fight).

**Skip when:** Triggers are spiky or accidental.

### Ascendancy / late Unique spikes

**Usually means:** Luxury ceilings. High ceiling, high greed risk.

**Take when:** The run is already stable and the Unique’s condition is your main verb.

**Skip when:** Act pressure is still punishing your opener. Stabilize first ([shop order](/guides/shop-order-shards)).

### Quest / LegendaryQuest

**Usually means:** Power gated behind a quest or unlock line.

**Take when:** You can realistically finish the line this run.

**Skip when:** The quest is a second job you will not play. Dead quests are expensive paperweights.

### SingleBattleBoost

**Usually means:** Short-horizon power for the next hard fight.

**Take when:** You need to survive *this* spike node.

**Skip when:** You needed run-long scaling and just bought a sugar high.

### Enemy / ActBoss entries

**Usually means:** Encounter documentation in the database — not a normal draft pickup. Use them to plan formation and sustain, not as shop greed.

## Rarity is a price tag, not a skill score

Rough mental model for Demo shops:

| Rarity signal | Treat it as | Common mistake |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common / Rare | Enabler budget | Ignoring them for “worse” Epics |
| Epic | Real plan piece | Taking off-plan Epics for loot dopamine |
| Legendary / Unique | Ceiling or identity lock | Early Unique with dead conditions |
| Quest / Boost | Conditional tools | Buying the sticker, not the schedule |

A Common Rush enabler that fires every fight beats a Unique on-kill piece you proc twice per act.

## Keyword honesty check

Open the relic tags and ask the rude questions:

- **Rush** — Who starts Rush windows every combat? ([Rush hub](/keywords/rush))
- **Stall** — Do fights last long enough for Stall payoffs? ([Stall](/keywords/stall))
- **Crit** — Do we stack Crit, or is this a random adjective? ([Crit](/keywords/crit))
- **Shield / Heal** — Who generates Shields, or are we hoping the relic alone tanks?
- **Class tags** — Do we have a real [Warrior](/classes/warrior) / [Assassin](/classes/assassin) / [Mystic](/classes/mystic) core, or one badge?

If you cannot answer without coping, the offer is not for this board.

## Worked comparisons

### Offer A: Assassin banner vs shiny Unique Crit

You have two Assassins and shaky Rush. The banner extends Assassin Rush. The Unique needs kills you do not secure yet.

**Read:** Banner is the enabler; Unique is a future spike. Take the banner (or Rank/placement first if openers fail).

### Offer B: Stall Shield engine vs Rush Ascendancy

Your board is Nyx / Irini Rush race. Stall engine wants long fights.

**Read:** Off-tempo. Skip even if rarity is higher. Buy Rush support or Rank the carry.

### Offer C: Filigree when banner already online

You already trigger the class keyword every fight. Challenge node next.

**Read:** Filigree/prism is correct spike timing. This is what mid rarities are for.

### Offer D: LegendaryQuest with no quest progress

**Read:** Skip unless the unlock is trivial from here. Quest text is part of the price.

## How to use relic pages on this site

On any relic detail page:

1. Read the effect once for **triggers** (on kill, on Rush, allies, permanent stacks).
2. Read **Shop decision** — Best for / Skip when / Shop note — as the short verdict.
3. Skim **Synergies** for tags you already own, not as a shopping list of everything shiny.
4. Only then compare rarity.

That loop is faster than memorizing 400 names.

## FAQ

### Are all banners good?

No. Banners are good on committed boards and bad as souvenirs. One class hero does not pay a banner.

### Should I ever take a Unique early?

When it *is* your enabler or the only stabilizer available — uncommon. Most early Uniques are skip-when traps with prestige lighting.

### What if two offers both match my keywords?

Prefer the one with the **higher trigger rate** on your current board, then the one that fixes the next node (survive vs spike). Rarity breaks ties last.

### Enemy relics show up in the database — can I draft them?

Treat Enemy / ActBoss rows as reference for what packs do. Your shop priority stays on draftable engines, banners, and enablers.

### Does this replace looking at builds?

[Builds](/builds) suggest a target relic set. This guide stops you from grabbing the right *names* at the wrong *time* or with the wrong *conditions*.

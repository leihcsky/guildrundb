In August we focused on making Guildrun Hub read like a **decision site**, not a raw sheet dump.

## Editorial and navigation

- Expanded [class playbooks](/classes) with how-to-play notes, shop priorities, and FAQs.
- Added contextual [guides](/guides) links from builds and the homepage featured list.
- Strengthened [About](/about) with operator contact and editorial standards.

## Database hygiene

- Public item/relic lists hide unfinished or disabled sheet rows (test entries, boosters, empty stubs).
- Thin relic and item detail pages stay available for lookup, but they are not treated as primary indexed content.
